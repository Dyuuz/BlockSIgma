What each column means

Current Price
Latest traded price.
Predicted Price
Model’s target price at the prediction horizon.
Price @ Predicted Time
Actual price observed at the prediction time (if elapsed).
Δ Now (%)
Percent change from prediction anchor to the current moment.
Δ @ Predicted (%)
Percent change measured exactly at the predicted time.
Prediction Status
High-level signal (e.g., “Buy to Buy - Reached”, “No action”).
Achievement
Whether the prediction condition was reached.
Dynamic TP / SL
Real-time trailing take-profit / stop-loss thresholds (%).
RRR
Risk-Reward Ratio (positive is favorable).
SL Status
Whether stop-loss is currently triggered.
Price Change Status
Auxiliary boolean indicating notable price move condition.
Times
Predicted/Expiry/Reached timestamps in your local timezone.
