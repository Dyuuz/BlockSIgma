What each column means

Current Price
Live market price.
Predicted Price
Expected market value at prediction timeframe.
Price @ Predicted Time
Actual price observed at the prediction time.
Δ Now (%)
Shows whether the coin has gained or lost value since the forecast point.
Δ @ Predicted (%)
Shows how far the prediction is from the asset price at the predicted time.
Prediction Status
Shows whether the forecast suggests a Buy opportunity.
Achievement
Indicates whether the prediction has successfully played out.
Dynamic TP
An adaptive profit target slightly below predicted price for safer exits.
Dynamic SL
An auto-adjusting stop loss tied to volatility and prediction distance.
RRR
A higher RRR means better trade efficiency; lower RRR means higher risk exposure.
SL Status
Helps track if stop-loss protection is strengthening over time.
Price Status
Shows if the price is higher than the last recorded price.
Predicted Time
The timestamp when the system generated the forecast.
Expiry Time
The cutoff point when the prediction is no longer valid.
