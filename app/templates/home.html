How BlockSigma Works

1

Data Collection

The pipeline ingests live quotes and ~475 hours of 30m/1hr OHLCV data from Binance, then computes core indicators (RSI, MACD). Symbols are enriched with human-readable names from CoinGecko, and data is cleaned/typed for modeling.

2

Prediction Analysis

Close prices are scaled and fed into a compact LSTM (10-step lookback, 50 units, 10 epochs) to forecast the next close. The prediction is compared to the current market price to label signals (Buy/No action) and derive percent gaps, dynamic take-profit/stop-loss thresholds, and risk-reward ratio.

3

Results Delivery

For each asset, this system provides a JSON record containing prices, deltas, statuses, and metrics, plus static UTC timestamps for when the prediction was made and when it expires (now + 4h/12hr). The function returns an array ready to store or serve to your dashboard.
